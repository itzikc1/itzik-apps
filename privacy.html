Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

"Itzik Apps Marketing" is a private tool used by one person, the owner of Itzik Apps, to publish content about our own apps to our own TikTok account.

What we access

The tool signs in to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit. It reads basic profile information (user.info.basic) and uploads and publishes our own videos and photos (video.upload, video.publish).

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data of visitors or other TikTok users. The only data stored is the owner's own access token, kept privately on the owner's computer.

Third parties

Content is sent only to TikTok. We do not share data with other third parties.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok settings, which also deletes the stored token.
